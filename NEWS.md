# gghockey 0.1.0

* First release: plotting functions pulled out of my private aRno package, which
  runs my hockey data pipeline. Includes `annotate_rink()`, `theme_rink()`, IIHF / NHL dimensions and
  `rink_dimensions()`, `rink_geometry()`, `geom_rink_bin()` / `rink_bin()` with
  matching `scale_*_rink()` scales, `team_palette()` with team colour scales,
  and the simulated `shots` dataset.
