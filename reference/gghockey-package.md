# gghockey: Ice Hockey Rinks and Spatial Heatmaps for 'ggplot2'

Draws to-scale ice hockey rinks (IIHF and NHL dimensions, or your own)
as 'ggplot2' layers, in horizontal or vertical orientation and cropped
to any combination of zones. Adds a binned heatmap geom for on-ice event
data whose squares are sized in rink units, so a map looks the same at
any export size, plus matched colour, size and alpha scales, a rink
theme, team colour palettes and optional centre-ice logos.
